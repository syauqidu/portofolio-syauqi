## Project overview

PC Builder is a website design concept for helping people plan a PC configuration before buying computer components. The goal was to make the planning experience more structured and understandable.

I contributed as a UI/UX designer in a three-person team. Our work followed a Design Thinking approach, from understanding users to developing and evaluating an interactive prototype.

## My role and the team's scope

My role was UI/UX Designer within the team. The process below describes our collective design work, from research and problem framing to interface design and evaluation.

The project covered UI/UX design and prototyping. Its deliverable was a proposed website experience, with no frontend or backend implementation.

## Understanding the PC-planning experience

### User interviews and empathy mapping

Our team interviewed users to understand their needs, difficulties, and expectations when planning a PC build. We organized the research into an empathy map to represent the user perspective and inform the design process.

### Mapping the user journey

We created a user journey map to understand the planning experience as a sequence of steps. This helped us identify obstacles that could be addressed through design.

The empathy map and journey map gave us complementary ways to consider users' needs and the points where they encountered difficulty.

## Framing problems and shaping the flow

We used How Might We (HMW) questions to frame problems identified through the research. These questions guided exploration of possible design responses.

We then developed a user flow to describe the steps and interactions within the proposed website. This connected the problem framing to a more concrete navigation structure.

## From wireframes to an interactive prototype

Wireframes established the page structure, navigation, and arrangement of interface components. Our team developed an interactive prototype from the wireframes and interface designs to represent how users would move through the concept.

The prototype made the proposed experience available for design evaluation before any software implementation.

## Design evaluation and deliverables

We evaluated the prototype to identify possible improvements to the design. This case study describes that evaluation at the process level; no specific testing method, participant count, or measured result is reported.

The team's design work included:

- An empathy map and user journey map informed by interviews.
- HMW problem framing and a user flow.
- Wireframes and interface designs.
- An interactive prototype and design evaluation.

The outcome was a design proposal for PC-build planning. The prototype represented the intended interactions, rather than a deployed service or a working component recommendation system.
