## Project overview

Our three-person team developed a Starbucks mobile application redesign for the Digi X National Business Plan & UI/UX Case Competition 2023. We reviewed the existing application to identify opportunities to improve its user experience.

The proposal focused on simpler ordering, a clearer interface, personalized drink recommendation concepts, and more transparent nutritional information. It was an independent competition concept, not an official update to the Starbucks application.

## My role and team contribution

I contributed as a UI/UX designer within the team. The research, problem definition, solution exploration, and redesign described here represent our collective work for the competition.

## Research and problem definition

We interviewed users to understand their experiences, needs, and difficulties when using the application. Alongside our review of the existing app, this research informed the problems we chose to address.

Our team analyzed the findings, defined the main design problems, and explored solutions based on user needs. We then translated those ideas into the proposed interface and user experience.

## Proposed experience

### Simpler ordering

We proposed a more minimal interface with clearer navigation and a simpler ordering flow. The design aimed to reduce interaction complexity and make ordering more comfortable to follow.

These were design objectives. No measured reduction in ordering time is claimed.

### Personalized chatbot recommendations

The redesign included a chatbot concept for recommending drinks based on factors such as:

- The user's mood.
- Purchase history or buying habits.
- Weather conditions.

The chatbot was a proposed feature within the competition design. It was not an implemented AI integration or a running production service.

### Clearer nutritional information

We proposed making sugar content more visible on drink menus. The intention was to help customers understand the nutritional information available and make more informed purchase decisions.

This was a transparency goal for the interface, rather than a claim of a proven change in customer behavior.

## Competition achievement

The project earned **3rd Place in the Digi X National Business Plan & UI/UX Case Competition 2023**, organized by **HIMA Bisnis Digital, Universitas Padjadjaran**, in **November 2023**.

The competition result recognizes the team's submission. The case study presents the research and proposed redesign within that competition scope.
